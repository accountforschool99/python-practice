# Unit 3 Chapter 3 Question 3 - Happy Holidays

Use a for loop that counts down to the holidays starting from $N$, which is a user-inputted number of days before the holidays. 

Execute the program normally, without making the program count down in real-time.

## Input Specification
It is guaranteed that $1 \le N \le 100$.

## Output Specification
Every line from 15 - 2 days should read "`[x] days until the holidays!`", with $x$ being a number starting at 15 and decreasing by 1 every time the loop runs. When $x$ is $1$, output `1 day until the holidays!`, to account for the singular form. 

The program should also output `Happy holidays!` when the countdown reaches `0`, without printing `0 days until the holidays!`

## Sample Input 1

```
10
```

## Sample Output 1

```
10 days until the holidays!
9 days until the holidays!
8 days until the holidays!
7 days until the holidays!
6 days until the holidays!
5 days until the holidays!
4 days until the holidays!
3 days until the holidays!
2 days until the holidays!
1 day until the holidays!
Happy holidays!
```
